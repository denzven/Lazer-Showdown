## 📜 The Code of the Grid

If you are reading this, you are about to face the Ser commanders in their own simulation. To them, this isn't just a game—it's a battlefield. Memorize these laws if you want to survive.

### ⏳ The Flow of Battle
Every engagement is fought over **Two Sets**. Each Set consists of **Three Rounds**. 
A Round is simple: the **Defender** maneuvers first, then the **Attacker** strikes. 
After the first Set, you switch sides. The commander with the highest total score at the end of both Sets claims victory.

### ⚙️ The Setup Phase
Before the first shot is fired, a **Toss** (highest die roll) decides who takes which role. 
* **The Defender** deploys three vital assets (worth 20, 30, and 50 points) anywhere on the grid—except the four corners.
* **The Attacker** then positions their primary weapon—the *Lzrshuby* (Lazer Piece)—in one of the four corners, aiming down any path.

### 🎲 Tactical Action Points (AP)
At the start of your turn, roll two dice. The sum is your available **Action Points**. You must spend them wisely:
* **MOVE (1 AP):** The Attacker can shift the Lazer Piece one square (Horizontal/Vertical). The Defender can scramble one of their Point Pieces one square.
* **ROTATE (1 AP):** The Attacker re-orients the Lazer Piece by 90 degrees to find a new firing line.
* **LAZER PRESS (1 AP):** The Attacker agitates the *Lzrshuby*, unleashing a devastating beam. The laser bounces 90 degrees off any *Mirlug* shells (Mirrors) and vaporizes any Point Piece it hits.

*(Note: In the heat of battle, you can perform these actions in any order. Unused AP is lost to the chaos of war.)*

### ⚡ The Desperation Challenge
If the Attacker is ruthless enough to capture all 3 pieces before the Set ends, they may declare a **Challenge**:
1. The Attacker nominates one vaporized piece.
2. Both commanders roll a single die.
3. **If the Attacker wins:** The Defender must frantically redeploy all pieces in new positions. If the Attacker captures the nominated piece again, they gain its points twice!
4. **If the Attacker loses:** Their hubris is punished, and the nominated piece's value is deducted from their score.

## ⚔️ The Theaters of War (Game Modes)

Over the years, the conflict evolved, creating different environments for the Showdown.

### 1. Standard Mode
The classic asymmetrical warfare. One commander hunts, the other evades. This is the pure form of the "La-Sir-She-Dan" ritual.

### 2. Pivot Point
A chaotic battlefield where the terrain shifts. The mirrors are no longer fixed; by spending **1 AP**, a commander can physically pivot a *Mirlug* shell in place, altering the entire grid's reflection network on the fly.

### 3. Trench Run
A bloody, symmetrical deathmatch. There are no defenders here. Both commanders place their own Lazer and Point Pieces in their home trenches. Both can fire, and friendly fire is disabled (the creatures recognize their own). The objective? Total annihilation. Destroy the enemy's pieces before they destroy yours.
